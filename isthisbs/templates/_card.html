{# Check card (DESIGN.md §5). Three variants: "" — default feed card: kicker, headline, claim+pill line. "row" — compact list row (section blocks, feeds): same anatomy, tighter. "headline" — headline stack (the Fresh Checks rail): kicker + linked finding ONLY — no claim, no pill, no clamp. The invariants allow it: only the finding may stand alone, and with no verdict shown there is nothing to mis-bind. Everywhere else the headline is the KEY FINDING (our reporting); the verdict pill rates the CLAIM, so it always sits on the same line as the quoted claim (invariant 1 — a pill may never sit alone under the finding).
